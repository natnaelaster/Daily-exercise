# 📊 E-commerce Sales Dashboard

**Python · Tableau | Sales Performance & Business Intelligence**

---

## 🎯 The Business Problem

An online retail business wanted to better understand its sales performance and customer purchasing behavior. While thousands of transactions were being recorded, the business lacked clear insights into:

* Which months generate the highest sales?
* Which products contribute the most revenue?
* Which countries drive the majority of sales?
* How do product returns affect overall performance?
* How can sales data be transformed into an interactive dashboard for business decision-making?

This project transforms raw transactional sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that provides actionable insights into sales trends, product performance, and geographic distribution.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

### 📅 Strong Seasonality

Sales peak during **November and December**, contributing more than **25% of total annual revenue**. This indicates strong seasonal demand, suggesting businesses should increase inventory and marketing efforts during the holiday period.

### 🌍 Revenue is Highly Concentrated

The **United Kingdom accounts for over 84% of total revenue**, making it the company's primary market while highlighting opportunities for geographic expansion.

### 🛒 Product Revenue Distribution

Although thousands of products are sold, the **Top 5 products generate approximately 7% of total revenue**, demonstrating that revenue is distributed across a wide product catalog rather than relying on only a few products.

### 🔄 Product Returns Impact Sales

Returned orders (negative quantities) represent **more than 2% of all transactions**, providing valuable insight into return behavior and its potential impact on inventory management and profitability.
